The scenario

The team needs clarity before it commits effort.

The procurement contains administrative requirements, specialist assertions, evidence requests and commercial inputs. Without an early control structure, contributors can start drafting before ownership and dependencies are understood.

The objective is not to make technical decisions for the supplier. It is to expose what must be answered, who must own it and what needs escalation.

Our approach

Turn uncertainty into a controlled response plan.

Extract

Separate mandatory requirements, requested evidence, dates and response instructions.

Assign

Route technical, privacy, insurance and commercial inputs to the appropriate client owners.

Flag

Identify assumptions, unresolved dependencies and items requiring clarification.

Control

Create a working requirements register that can drive drafting and review.
